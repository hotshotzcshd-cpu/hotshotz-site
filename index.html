Meet the Founder

South side kid, Arizona hot dog stand.

The owner and founder of Hot Shotz Chicago Style Hotdogs is Ryan Pilota. I grew up on the south side of Chicago, and for many years the Chicago style hot dog was a staple at any barbecue or sporting event. Once I moved out here to Arizona, it was difficult to find a really good Chicago style hot dog — hence the reason we started this small food truck business.

The Loki story is kind of funny: we were having a barbecue at our home, and Loki was just a puppy at the time. He literally devoured both of my Chicago style hot dogs right off my plate. At that point, a lightbulb went off — I pictured him basically swooping in and eating a hot dog, and that's what started this whole thing.

I look forward to meeting you, and I'd love to swap stories if you're from Chicago too.
